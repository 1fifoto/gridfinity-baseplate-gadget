Machining tools

Roughing allowance mm
If the roughing allowance is greater than zero, the finish pass includes a pocket toolpath and a profile toolpath. If the roughing allowance is zero, the finish pass includes only a profile toolpath.
Roughing end mill
Finishing end mill
45° chamfer V-bit
The required cutter has a 45° cutting edge, represented in Vectric as a 90° included-angle V-bit, no larger than 1/4" (6.35 mm) in diameter. A 1/2" V-bit is not supported. Tool database feeds, speeds, stepdown, and stepover are retained.
Inch tools are supported. With the lower chamfer omitted, the machined floor has a 1.6 mm plan-view corner radius and a 1/8" finishing end mill fits.

Standard Gridfinity cells are 42 mm × 42 mm. These values also define the X and Y grid pitch.
Machined pocket profile: 42 mm × 42 mm cell; 2.15 mm upper chamfer; straight wall from 2.15 mm to the 4.65 mm terminal socket depth. The referenced 0.7 mm lower chamfer is currently omitted. Magnet sub-pockets continue below this floor only when enabled.
