The required cutter has a 45° cutting edge, represented in Vectric as a 90° included-angle V-bit. Tool database feeds, speeds, stepdown, and stepover are retained.
With zero roughing allowance, finishing profiles only the vertical walls. A positive allowance also produces a finishing raster across the floor.
Inch tools are supported. With the lower chamfer omitted, the machined floor has a 1.6 mm plan-view corner radius and a 1/8" finishing end mill fits.
