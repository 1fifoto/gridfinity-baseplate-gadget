The required cutter has a 45° cutting edge, represented in Vectric as a 90° included-angle V-bit. Tool database feeds, speeds, stepdown, and stepover are retained.
Inch tools are supported. The standard 0.9 mm bottom corner radius requires a finishing end mill no larger than 1.8 mm; a 1/16" cutter works, while a 1/8" cutter does not reproduce that corner.

Four round pockets are placed in each cell. The finishing end mill clears them and the V-bit cuts their optional top chamfer.
Negative pocket profile: 42 mm × 42 mm cell; 2.15 mm upper chamfer; 1.8 mm vertical wall; 0.7 mm lower chamfer; 4.65 mm terminal socket depth. Magnet sub-pockets continue below this floor only when enabled.
