Creates Gridfinity CNC geometry and editable native toolpaths. All dimensions are millimeters. The machined pocket profile uses a 42 mm × 42 mm cell, a 2.15 mm upper chamfer, and a straight wall from 2.15 mm to the 4.65 mm terminal socket depth. The referenced 0.7 mm lower chamfer is currently omitted. Magnet sub-pockets continue below this floor only when enabled.
